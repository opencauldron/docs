Your profile is your account page in the studio. It shows who you are to your team, tracks your progress, and holds a few personal preferences. Open it from your avatar menu, or go to **Profile** in the sidebar — you land on your own profile, with an **Edit profile** button only you can see.

Teammates can open your profile too. They see your name, level, badges, and recent work, but not your preferences.

## Edit your name and avatar

Click **Edit profile** to open the editor.

- **Display name** — the name shown across the studio, on your profile, and in threads. Up to 80 characters.
- **Avatar** — click **Upload image** to pick a photo. Use PNG, JPEG, or WebP, up to 2 MB. Click **Replace image** to swap it.

Click **Save changes**. Your new name and avatar update everywhere right away, including the sidebar.

## Preferences

The **Preferences** section is yours alone. Each toggle saves on its own.

| Preference | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Notify me when generations finish** | When on, a notification lands in the bell (top-right) every time one of your generations finishes or fails. The Queue pill next to **+ Create** already tracks in-flight work; this adds a record you can come back to |
| **Show my stats when I sign in** | When on, your home page opens with a short snapshot of your progress — XP, level, and what you've made this week — above the rest. Turn it off to skip straight to your widgets |

If your studio admin has enabled mature content, you'll see an optional toggle.

## Stats, level, and badges

The top of your profile sums up your activity:

- **Member since** — the month you joined.
- **Day streak** — how many days in a row you've created something.
- **Level and title** — your current level and the title that comes with it, with a ring showing your progress toward the next one.
- **Generations** — how many images and video you've made, all time.
- **Favorite** — the model you reach for most.

Below the stats, the **Feats** grid shows every badge in the studio. Earned ones are lit up with the date you got them; the rest stay locked until you meet them. For how levels, XP, and feats work, see [XP, levels & feats](/guides/xp-and-feats/).

To track what counts toward your level and limits, see [Usage & limits](/guides/usage-and-limits/).

## Recent creations

The **Recent Creations** grid shows your latest images and video. Click any one to open it in your [Library](/guides/library/).
