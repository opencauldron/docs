---
title: Finding assets
description: Search your whole library by words or by look, surface visual matches with Find similar, and narrow with filters
---

A growing library can hold thousands of images and video. The search box, the search-ranking setting, **Find similar**, and the filters work together to get you to the right asset fast. Everything here works in [The Library](/guides/library/), across both uploaded files and generated media.

## Search the library

Type in the **Search assets…** box at the top of the Library. Results update as you type. Press `/` anywhere on the page to jump to the box, and press `Esc` to clear it.

What search matches depends on the ranking mode you pick — see below.

## Choose how results rank

Next to the search box is a ranking control that shows the current mode (the default reads **Hybrid**). Open it to switch between three ways of ordering results:

| Mode | What it does | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Text** | Ranks by the exact words in prompts, filenames, and tags | Finding an asset when you remember its name, a tag, or wording from the prompt |
| **Hybrid** | Blends word matching with how an image looks — the default | Most searches, where you want both wording and visual match to count |
| **Similar** | Ranks by how an image looks, not by exact words | Describing a look you can't name precisely (a color, a mood, a composition) |

A few things to know:

- **Hybrid** is the default and covers most searches. Switch only when you want to favor exact wording (**Text**) or favor look (**Similar**).
- Ranking applies once you type a search. With an empty box, the mode you pick is set aside for your next search.
- Your choice sticks for the session, so you can run several searches the same way.

## Find similar

To find assets that look like one you're already viewing, open any asset to its detail panel and click **Find similar**. The grid switches to a **Similar assets** view, ordered by how close each match looks to the one you started from, with the heading "Nearest matches to" that asset.

**Find similar** works on both uploaded and generated assets, which makes it useful for:

- De-duplicating a photo shoot — spot near-identical frames and keep the best one.
- Finding variants — gather every take on the same concept that's scattered across the library.
- Building a set — pull together assets that share a look without hunting by name.

To leave the view and return to your normal grid, click **Back to library**.

Two messages can appear instead of matches:

- **Still indexing** — the asset was added very recently and isn't ready for visual search yet. It's usually ready within a few minutes; check back shortly.
- **No similar assets** — nothing else in your library looks close to this one yet. Add more assets and try again.

## Narrow with filters

Filters cut a long result list down to what you need. Combine them with search, or use them on their own to browse.

- **Brand** — the brand selector in the top bar scopes the whole Library to one brand's assets. See [Brands](/guides/brands/).
- **Campaign** — show only assets tagged to a campaign.
- **Tags** — match one or more tags. Switch the match mode between **Any (OR)** (assets with any selected tag) and **All (AND)** (assets with every selected tag).
- **Collection** — show only assets in a collection.

Open **More** for the rest (on a narrow screen the whole filter set collapses into one **Filters** button):

| Filter | Options |
|---|---|
| **Source** | **Uploaded**, **Generated**, **Imported** |
| **Status** | **Draft**, **In review**, **Approved**, **Changes requested**, **Archived** |
| **Rating** | One to five stars; pick more than one to match any of them |

## Narrow by date

When you know roughly *when* something landed, the Library's date controls are faster than search. **Timeline** opens an upload-volume chart you can drag across to pick a range (with **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, and **This month** presets), and the **Jump to** rail skips straight to a period. Date range combines with everything above, and it travels in the page link so you can share the exact slice. See [The Library](/guides/library/) for the full tour.

For how to add tags, collections, and ratings so assets are findable later, see [Uploading & organizing](/guides/uploading/).
