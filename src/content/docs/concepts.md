---
title: How Cauldron fits together
description: A plain-language map of how your studio, brands, library, and review pipeline relate to each other
---

Cauldron has a few core pieces. Once you know how they nest, the rest of the product follows. This page is the map. Each term links to its full guide.

## The shape at a glance

```
Studio (your team's shared workspace)
│
├─ Brand — one per client, division, or project
│   ├─ Kit ......... the brand's style rules
│   ├─ Members ..... people, with roles
│   └─ Campaigns ... groupings by initiative
│
├─ Library — every uploaded asset and AI generation
│   ├─ Collections .. folders you file work into
│   └─ Tags ......... free-form labels
│
└─ Review — Draft → In review → Approved
```

Read top to bottom: your **Studio** contains **Brands**; each brand has a **Kit**, **Members**, and **Campaigns**; the work itself lives in the **Library**; and team work moves through **Review** before it's final.

## Studio

Your **Studio** is your team's home — the shared workspace that everything else lives inside. Teammates are invited into the studio and given a studio role: Owner, Admin, or Member. What someone can do inside a particular brand is set separately by their brand role. To invite people and set studio roles, see [Members](/settings/members/).

## Brands

A **Brand** is one client, division, or project. You create as many as you need, and everyone also gets a private **Personal** brand to use as a scratch space. Brands keep each client's work, style, and people separate. See [Brands](/guides/brands/).

Each brand holds three things:

- **Kit** — the brand's style rules. Prompt text added to every generation, banned terms, a color, a logo, and a default model add-on. The Kit shapes what the brand produces. See [Brands](/guides/brands/).
- **Members** — the people on the brand and what each can do. Brand roles are Manager, Creator, and Viewer. A teammate can hold different roles on different brands. See [Members](/settings/members/).
- **Campaigns** — groupings by initiative, like a launch or a seasonal push. Campaigns sort a brand's work for filtering and reporting, and can be shared as a public gallery. See [Campaigns](/guides/campaigns/).

## Library

The **Library** is your digital asset manager — one place for every brand asset your team uploads (shoots, logos, kits) and every image and video you create. See [Library](/guides/library/).

You organize the Library two ways:

- **Collections** — folders you file work into. They nest, so you can build a structure like `2026 Launch / Hero shots`. See [Finding assets](/guides/finding-assets/).
- **Tags** — free-form labels you attach to any asset for quick filtering across collections. See [Library](/guides/library/).

The same Library serves both kinds of work. Uploaded brand assets and AI generations sit side by side, filterable by brand, campaign, collection, tag, and status.

## Review

**Review** is how team work becomes final. An asset moves through three states: **Draft** (you're still working), **In review** (submitted for a decision), and **Approved** (signed off). A brand Manager either approves a submission or sends it back marked **Changes requested**. See [Review](/guides/review/).

Work on your Personal brand stays as a draft and skips review — only assets on a team brand can be submitted.

## Assistant

The **Assistant** is an in-app AI helper that knows your Library and your brands, so you can ask for what you want in plain language — find an asset, generate an image, or edit one you point it at.

## Tools

**Tools** are one-click operations on a single image, with no prompt to write: upscale it, remove its background, or reframe it for another aspect ratio. Results save back into your Library beside the original.
