title: Studio settings

Your **Studio** is your team's shared workspace. Studio settings cover the basics everyone sees: the studio name, its logo, and the studio-wide control over mature content.

Open these under **Settings → Studio**. The page is owner and admin only. If you're a studio Member, you'll see a note asking you to contact a studio Owner or Admin to make changes.

## General

The General section holds settings that are visible to everyone in your studio.

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The studio name shown in the sidebar and across the app. Required, up to 80 characters |
| **Logo URL** | A link to an image to use as your studio logo. Optional — leave it blank to use the default logo |

To update either:

1. Go to **Settings → Studio**.
2. Edit the **Name** or paste a web address into **Logo URL**.
3. Click **Save changes**.

The **Save changes** button stays disabled until you change something, and the logo link has to be a valid web address (or empty). After you save, the new name and logo appear in the sidebar.

## Content

The Content section has one studio-wide control: **Allow mature content**.

This is the master switch for your whole studio. It's off by default. Turning it on doesn't change any brand on its own — it only makes the option available. When it's on, brand Managers can opt individual brands in, which lets creators relax the safety filter on supported models for that brand. Some models still moderate certain content regardless of this setting.

For most teams, leave this off. Follow your own content guidelines when you decide.

## Who can change these

Studio settings are limited to studio Owners and Admins. Members can sign in and work on their brands, but they can't edit the studio name, logo, or the mature-content switch.

- To add people, set studio roles, or manage each brand's Managers, Creators, and Viewers, see [Members & roles](/settings/members/).
- For studio-wide controls beyond these settings, see [Admin tools](/guides/admin/).
