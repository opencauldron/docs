Cauldron lives at **studio.opencauldron.ai** — there's nothing to download or install. You sign in with your Google account, answer a few quick questions the first time, and land on your **Getting started** board.

:::note
Cauldron is in **invite-only beta**. If you don't have access yet, request an invite at [opencauldron.ai](https://opencauldron.ai). Once you've been invited, sign in with Google below.
:::

## Sign in with Google

1. Go to **studio.opencauldron.ai**.
2. The page opens with a **Sign in** / **Sign up** toggle. Pick **Sign up** if this is your first time, or **Sign in** to return.
3. Click **Sign in with Google** (or **Sign up with Google**) and choose your Google account.

You may also see a magic-link option. If an email field appears, type your address and click **Email me a magic link**, then open the link from your inbox.

While the beta is invite-only, sign in with the Google account your invite was sent to. If you try an account that hasn't been granted access yet, you'll see an **Access denied** message — use the invited address, or ask whoever invited you to add it.

## First-time setup

The first time you sign in, Cauldron asks for a few details so it can set up your account. This takes under a minute.

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **What should we call you?** | Your display name — how teammates see you in comments, threads, and activity |
| **Name your studio** | The name for your Studio, your team's shared workspace (you can rename it later in Studio Settings) |
| **What best describes you?** | Your role: Agency owner, Brand marketer, Freelancer, Hobbyist, or Other |
| **How much AI media have you made?** | Your experience level: Brand new, Dabbled, Weekly, or Power user |
| **What brings you here?** | Optional — a sentence about what you want to do |

A Studio is your team's shared workspace. Everything you create, every brand, and every teammate you invite lives inside it.

Tick the box to agree to the **Terms of Service** and **Privacy Policy**, then click **Create studio**. You land on your **Getting started** board.

### If you were invited to an existing Studio

When a teammate invites you, you join their Studio instead of creating your own. The setup screen welcomes you to that Studio and skips the studio-naming step — you only confirm your display name, role, and experience level. Click **Enter studio** and you land on the **Getting started** board for the team you joined.

## Where you land

After setup, the **Getting started** board walks you through your first steps: creating a brand, generating your first image, and inviting teammates. It's optional and you can dismiss it any time.

From there:

- [Getting started](/getting-started/) walks through your first brand and first creation.
- [How Cauldron fits together](/concepts/) explains Studios, brands, the Library, and review in one place.

## Signing in later

To return, go to **studio.opencauldron.ai** and click **Sign in with Google**. You skip setup and go straight to your home screen.

If you belong to more than one Studio, you switch between them from the Studio menu once you're in.
